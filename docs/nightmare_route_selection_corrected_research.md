## Separate Hidden/fire mechanics diagnostic

The client's `RenderableUnit.ts` draws the Hidden buff at 0.4 opacity: it marks untargetability rather
than concealing the occupied cells. Ordinary movement geometry remains public. This study's conservative
enemy filter excludes Hidden units as pressure targets, while retaining known own Hidden allies.

The separate `hidden-fire-mechanics.ts` fixture runs against the frozen common source. A 1,000-HP ground
stack crossing one 25% wall cell takes **250 HP**, ending at 750 HP with two bodies lost, both with and
without the Hidden buff. The read-only movement projection and authoritative burn helper agree exactly.
Hidden is therefore not Fire Wall immunity. Considering these known untargetable ground units for a
ground-effect spell is a distinct possible coverage improvement, not a measured strength result. This
diagnostic does not modify the registered candidate, its recorded exclusion, fresh comparisons or gates.

## Closed disposition and next research boundary

Keep both wall candidates inactive and preserve this opened bank, all rejected/unused reservations,
the first failed admission, the corrected integration tests and the complete evidence. The accepted
A19 pair, adopted barrel correction, default 0.9 and Premium remain unchanged. Do not reopen this bank,
relax its gates or promote a positive slice. The
[corrected evidence fixture](evidence/nightmare_route_selection_corrected_20261117.json) contains every
paired result, family score, outcome-changing trace summary, source hash and verification receipt.

The movement cases show that an opportunity-cost rule must account for movement as well as attacks
and Empower. The route cases show that one-turn distance preservation is an incomplete proxy for
three-lap fire value. The earlier frozen encoder diagnostic also established that A19 cannot distinguish
existing fire cells/lifetime/power or wall orientation in its learned input. These are concrete next
hypotheses, not a claim that any particular replacement will win. A new wall-aware representation needs
a distinct input contract, fits, untouched evaluation banks, both current-default and original-wall
comparators, independent confirmation and serial serving-cost checks. Any smaller policy experiment
likewise needs a new registration and fresh evidence.
