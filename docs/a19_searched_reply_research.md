# A19: two bounded searched enemy replies

Registered October 31, 2026. **The fresh development test fails; this policy is
closed without opening cost, qualification or confirmation.** The neural-guided
R8 acquisition rule and its development evidence remain unchanged. No new serving
default is enabled.

The [previous complete loss audit](a19_six_unit_precision_research.md) finds that
all 34 matched R2 alternatives enter R8's evaluated banks. Selected forecasts
also miss actual searched responses: native Dryad chooses Troll while real R2
chooses and kills Fairy on that reply state. That motivates testing a better
opponent model, but does not establish that any particular approximation wins.

## Fixed policy

Start ordinary H64/complementary-R2/K6 directly at every searched candidate-team
turn. Inside each full turn-mode forecast, give the first two eligible enemy
turns an independent H8/R1/K2 choice from the projected public state. Eligibility
is non-AI-Driven v0.8, across all units, maps and artifacts. Child forecasts stay
native; own future turns and subsequent enemy turns keep the original model.
There is no R2 pilot, R8 teacher, fitted-model trigger, recorded future action,
unit-name filter or outcome-derived exception.

The child restores full battle state, object aliases, target memory, statistics,
actor, RNG/UUID and unit references. Native-array identity governs target-memory
commit exactly as in the real battle loop. Search nesting is capped at one and
child horizon at eight. Every child call, leaf probe and simulated turn is
counted. Exceptions must surface even through the native strategy's catch path.
These settings are fixed before any game of this policy; no sweep follows a
negative result.
