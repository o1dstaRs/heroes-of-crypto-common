## Retained private UUID state differs

The registered exact retained-sample gate **fails**. In the live comparison, 54 retained samples across
`378-0-r2` and `380-2-r2` differ in their before/after UUID counters. All observed retained scores,
material values and target-memory hashes match. Offline samples are identical, and live candidate
samples remain an ordered subset. Both cold epochs reproduce the counterexamples exactly.

For example, the same retained sample in `378-0-r2` enters/exits at UUID counters 26/28 in the unpruned
live reference and 24/26 in the pruned implementation. Search restores the global sequence at the whole
decision boundary, explaining matching committed counters here; the sequence is not restored separately
before every private sample. A skipped sample can therefore affect later private allocation state.
This diagnostic does not prove those allocations caused an outcome or are harmless for every future ID.
