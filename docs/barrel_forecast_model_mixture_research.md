# Uncertain barrel forecasts within the same R2 budget

`barrel-forecast-paired-model-mixture-v1` is a distinct offline hypothesis after two closed failures:
[global native intentions](barrel_forecast_intent_research.md) and the
[friendly-shot clearing guard](barrel_forecast_useful_clear_research.md). Their source hashes,
registrations, original controls, losing cases and failed gates remain unchanged.

The global replacement changed ordinary shot targeting and movement decisions through its forecasts.
A hard firing-lane exception recovered some losses but removed more wins. This candidate represents
uncertainty between the two future policies instead of declaring either always correct. It does not
isolate a universal cause of the previous failures or claim maximin play.

## Fixed policy and cost contract

The canonical helper is `src/simulation/barrel_forecast_model_mixture_research.ts`. Importing it
installs no hook. At full canonical R2 comparisons, one existing sample uses native cleanup and the
other uses the exact closed v1 intent policy. Both projected armies and barrel owners use the same
model within a sample. Semantic root-seed parity balances which existing complementary chance stream
gets the intent model. Assignment is identical for every candidate at that root and consumes no RNG.
Root construction, leaf/reply/lap probes and configured R4 remain native. Invalid queries fail closed.

Each model receives **one chance sample**, not a complete antithetic pair of its own. The two old chance
streams are preserved, but within-model antithetic cancellation is not. Strength must be measured;
there is no claim that averaging these approximations produces a calibrated value or a more accurate
opponent. Existing bounds, primary/material selection and final neural/support corrections remain
unchanged. No action, rollout, model inference, fitted weight, draft, Setup or placement rule is added.
The complete-decision/full-fight +10% cost gate still applies to any eventual promotion.

## Frozen reused admission

The [registration](evidence/barrel_forecast_model_mixture_registration_20261117.json) predates the first
candidate game. All 864 cases from the original v9 bank are included, including previously unchanged
outcomes. All 68 old regressions and 74 gains additionally rerun both original arms, requiring exact
input, full action/event trace and score parity: **1,148 executions**. Six nice-19 local workers share
a fixed 30-minute deadline; no automatic retry or extension is permitted.

Admission requires all engine/model/callback checks, both full-turn future models exercised, fewer
worsened outcomes versus stock than naive v1, and strictly higher total score than naive v1 across
all 864 cases. No unit/map/style/outcome subset or fitted mixture coefficient replaces the whole-bank
result. Five focused tests cover balanced/stable sample assignment, RNG purity, root/probe/R4 isolation,
protected actions, invalid-query fail-closed behavior and absence from all serving/closed source.

These are reused diagnostics, not a fresh strength estimate. Only passing opens a separate frozen
study on independent channels against the subsequently adopted v10 default, retaining the accepted
neural reference, complete uncertainty/map/doctrine/style gates and +10% serving-cost qualification.
No new qualification/confirmation channels are allocated and no serving default changes here.
