# Magic Dragon tactics research

`magic-dragon-tactics-v1` is opt-in, offline research. Importing its helper or the optional Meteor Shower candidate flag changes no default. The existing A19 draft, placement and adopted Satyr, Healer, Wandering Mage and Battle Mage support remain active.

The November 14 CPU campaign compares current A19 with wider distinct Meteor Shower impacts, a spell-diverse shortlist of unchanged size, four explicit first-spell diagnostics, protected rear placement and three split policies. Every fight uses locked V3 drafting and full v0.8/A19 combat on both armies, with actual server splits, formations and accepted barrels. It is conditional on the designated army drafting Magic Dragon; it is not native v0.9 combat or an overall ranked win-rate estimate.

Native splits clone complete spellbooks but do not preserve stack power. Power depends on the largest deployed experience stack across both armies. At a reference of 2,000 experience, four Dragons split into two pairs retain two usable Whirlpools but lose Ring of Fire and Meteor Shower. Two Dragons split into singles fall below Whirlpool's power threshold. Reflection also weakens with stack power. More scrolls alone therefore do not establish a better policy.

The split planner uses the own roster and already funded capacity. It preserves adopted Healer and Wandering Mage allocations, can displace at most one generic split and never buys Placement. The control-pair proposal estimates a power-three threshold from the own army; a larger enemy can still invalidate that estimate. Rear positioning uses complete legal footprints and retains the whole incumbent formation when it cannot place every Dragon. Ordinary engine rules retain Whirlpool's activation timing, elemental immunity, Ring of Fire's friendly splash and empty center, and Meteor Shower's enemy-only footprint.

The fixed study reserves 108 discovery families / 2,376 fights across 11 tactics, followed by 216 fresh qualification families / 1,296 fights and, only after passing, 432 independent confirmation families / 2,592 fights. Each family has two physical seats. Three maps, all nine doctrine pairings and both draft pick orders are balanced. Selection uses the complete discovery bank; its intervals cannot establish strength after selecting the best tactic. Both fresh comparisons against current A19 and the immutable full-policy 50.36% reference must gain at least 0.5 points, have positive whole-family 95% lower bounds and nonnegative means for every own doctrine. No pooling, refilling or replacement after fresh results.

The authoritative final action must be checked after A19's neural tie correction: an internal `SearchDriver` result can differ from the action actually executed. Full turn/event/committed-health records, original A/A replay and cross-host native setup parity are required. Failed instrumentation smoke records remain separate from strength games.
