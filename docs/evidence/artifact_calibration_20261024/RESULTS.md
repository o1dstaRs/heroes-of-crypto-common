The fixed fitted table fails the registered calibration gate. Keep the current artifact-fit-v6 policy.

## Complete sample

All 150 registered boards completed: 90 local and 60 remote, with no gaps or overlaps. Each evaluated
all three legally offered Tier-2 choices under both draft assignments and both physical sides, giving
1,800 full fights. There were zero rejected actions. The collector verified matching source, server
profile, protocol, runner, fitter and Bun identities across both complete ranges before fitting.

The five-fold fit used only the five own creatures visible at the Tier-2 decision to identify the
predeclared native-ranged cohort. 292/300 scenarios qualified. Artifact choices occurred at the actual
pick phase, allowing the ordinary subsequent level-four pick and setup to respond naturally. No future
pick, private opponent information or forced post-draft substitution entered the decision.
The out-of-fold policies changed 168/300 choices, so this was an active comparison rather than a null
caused by the candidate never being exercised.
