## Reproduction and disposition

Independent Python/Node checks verify all 2,817 source bindings, 660 content hashes, authoritative
winner journals, eighteen complete original controls, final body placement, paid levels, first-root
identity and all twelve changed-outcome repeats. A separate native replay reconstructs all 66
model envelopes and regenerates **all 528 proposals exactly**, without new battles.

The first replay failed because detached JSON sorts body keys. The existing proposal seed uses
`JSON.stringify` of body fields in native constructor order: semantic deep equality alone does not
preserve that private generator input. Rebuilding the envelope through `buildPlacementInput` restores
the original order and reproduces every proposal. Keep the original failed replay/source and this
contract in future dataset pipelines; do not regenerate live proposals directly from sorted JSON.
No proposal, battle, weight, gate or seed changed to pass the corrected replay.

A prior type-only import and Python launcher-syntax failure launched zero native games. Its complete
source/log/freeze is preserved. The separate packaging-v2 identity fixes those issues, keeps the
same six cases, seven alternatives and original 22:00 UTC cutoff, and passes strict TypeScript.

Complete source, all 72 full outcomes and root frames, failures, recipes and independent verifiers
remain in the client's ignored `game/core/tmp/a19-barrel-formation-opportunity-20261118` capsule.
The complete archive and every regular member were verified locally and on Puffalo; the fixture
binds both archive receipts. Current v13, its locked models and all earlier failed studies remain intact.

Future work can use this as exposed diagnostic evidence for proposal-value learning with public
