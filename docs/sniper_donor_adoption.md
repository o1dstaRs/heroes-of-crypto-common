tactical splits and placement lifecycle. No paid upgrade or new geometry is introduced.

distinct creatures per side, a visible ranged threat, at least two offensive own melee types,
the actual unused delivery draft allowance, a fully funded native plan and native Placement =
Movement = 0. Protection additionally requires native Sniper >= 2, a live Normal/Lava/Barrels map,
a 5/6/7-point budget and standard 1,000-XP own draft totals. Check own totals across split stacks;
do not inspect concealed enemy counts, doctrine, upgrades or positions to select a policy.

partial automatic spending, custom own quantities, Water and native Sniper 0/1 retain their

and rollback rules. Already funded v12 plans retain their saved formation after a restart;
new selections use the protected policy and a distinct cache identity.
