# Dual Strike draft candidate

`artifact-fit-v7:<base-setup-policy>` is an explicit candidate. The registered AI and the v1–v6 setup identities retain their existing behavior.

Dual Strike Charm amplifies the second hit, so its draft value depends on the army. At full second-hit power, two equal hits become `1 + 1.5` rather than `1 + 1`: a 25% increase in their combined physical output. The candidate recognizes the engine's shared Double Punch and Double Shot families, including Gargantuan's Double Throw. Ranged stacks require enough initial ammunition for both shots.

The opening bundle scores only its two known creatures. Later picks add a bounded preference for native second attackers when the army owns the Charm; the existing strength, faction, and role heads still participate. An ordinary unit with a substantially stronger base score can still win its offer.

Blacksmith is recognized from its native Craft charge. Craft's double-attack outcome has a 40% chance, and an existing native/crafted double cannot become a third attack. The forecast therefore values eligible single attackers, discounts the chance, and budgets for two recipients rather than assuming the entire army will fit a future Craft area. These are conservative draft features, not measured win rates or guaranteed combat outcomes.

The candidate changes only Dual Strike's Tier-1 fit and its associated draft affinity. Other Tier-1 fit scores delegate to v4, Tier-2 decisions retain v6, and augment spending, synergies, placement, and the combat search budget are unchanged. Explicit v6 remains available for comparisons and replay.

Use legal draft-board comparisons and then full A19 fights with the same combat profile on both seats. A diagnostic that selects changed boards without looking at outcomes estimates improvement where the feature acts; its score must not be presented as an unconditional ranked win rate. Keep source, seeds, setup identities, selected boards, physical-seat swaps, search budget, and rejected-action counters with the evidence. Fresh confirmation must retain the candidate parameters chosen before the first fight results.
