# Delivery setup: price the shooting investment

Status, November 18, 2026: **independently confirmed, unpromoted research checkpoint**
`a19-preserve-sniper-20261118`. Fresh development and held-out strength, source/state controls and
serial CPU qualification passed unchanged. No serving default, learned weights, client/server common
pin or production deployment changed. The accepted melee-barrel correction remains enabled.

The public delivery planner measures eligible melee contact with twelve hypothetical backline cells,
weighted by stack experience. Its fully funded Movement/Placement plans preserve Armor and Might but
can refund Sniper, retaining level one when shooters are present. The reach utility has no term for
the shooting investment it removes. Sniper three supplies +27% base attack and +70% shot distance;
Sniper one supplies +8% and +20%. These are engine stat bonuses, not measured damage-per-second gains.
A larger reach score therefore does not establish that one setup dominates the other.

The separate guard filters existing funded plans **before preview** when they reduce Sniper below the
fully funded native base. It retains native free-forward geometry, score thresholds, tactical splits,
terrain behavior, real budgets and A19 combat. It does not add a placement repair or use hidden enemy
deployment. It can give up useful melee contact; actual outcomes, not the source finding, decide
whether this is an improvement.
