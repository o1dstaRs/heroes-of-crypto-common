# Joint delivery contact and shooting utility

Status, November 18, 2026: **closed, failed reused-development screen; no default change**. The
single fixed candidate tied the qualified Sniper guard and failed its required incremental gate.
No fresh qualification, cost qualification or confirmation opened. The confirmed
`a19-preserve-sniper-20261118` reference remains unchanged. Read
`a19_delivery_donor_research.md` for its distinct fresh confirmation and CPU result.

The native delivery planner prices melee contact but not the shooting investment refunded to buy
Movement or Placement. Preserving native Sniper passed independent confirmation on its declared
public-Setup population, but paid movement still won some individual development cases. That tradeoff
motivates this separate utility hypothesis; it does not authorize an exception to the frozen guard.

The single fixed candidate is `a19-joint-contact-shooting-utility-v1`. Each native preview retains the
original contact utility and adds:

```
sum over own native shooters:
  (alive amount × experience / 1000)
  × (actual projected attack / printed attack)
  × mean(1 / engine ranged-attack divisor across twelve hypothetical backline cells)
```

The target cells are the native contact scorer's fixed opposing columns and rows, not observed enemy
deployment. The actual engine supplies square whole-cell range bands, rectangular attacker geometry,
the maximum divisor, and Sniper/Farsight range-ignore behavior. Projected own attack and range include
the real funded setup and native splits. Equal XP-unit weights, the existing paid margin of 0.25,
native catalog/order, terrain, formation, artifacts, combat search and learned weights remain fixed.
No coefficient is fitted, no new paid plan is introduced, and no draft override is added. The session
cache signature includes the research policy to prevent reuse of another policy's selected plan.

This is a heuristic utility, not predicted damage or a win probability. It does not model private
enemy defenses or deployment, shot interception, suppression, incoming damage, or survival during
arrival. Faster contact and better shooting projections must still repay their actual costs in full
fight outcomes.
